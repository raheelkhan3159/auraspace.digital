Stories Captured on the Run

Roadcast is AuraSpace's traveling mobile broadcast lab. We strip away static corporate studios and take cinema-grade rigs onto remote highways, muddy rally trails, and grueling dawn sets. It's unscripted dialogue with directors, stunt teams, and rebel creators who refuse ordinary production pipelines.

Each installment is cut right inside the support vehicle between shooting locations, balancing authentic audio ambiance with high-intensity visual rhythm.

Full Frame

Captured on dual cinema bodies with prime anamorphic glass.

Field Audio

32-bit float multi-mic arrays capturing authentic roaring engines.

Same-Day Cut

Graded on DaVinci Resolve rigs powered via mobile generator.

Production Spec

Self-Contained Broadcast Hub

High-speed satellite uplink and autonomous lithium-ion generators permit 14 hours of non-stop multi-camera capture in zero-cellular wilderness environments.
