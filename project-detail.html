Capturing the Chaos: Derajat Off-Road Challenge 2025

Mud Flew, Engines Roared, Legends Born

About the Project

Mud flew, engines roared, and legends were born at the ultimate Off-Road Challenge in DI Khan. When the Directorate General of Sports KP came to us to cover the 2025 Derajat Jeep Rally, we knew standard event coverage wasn't going to cut it. This is a visceral, adrenaline-fueled battle of man and machine against nature.

From daring climbs to heart-stopping drops, these warriors conquered every obstacle with raw power and unstoppable spirit. Our mission was to put the viewer right in the passenger seat. We didn't just document a race; we captured the dirt, the speed, and the absolute chaos of the track, turning a regional sports event into a high-end cinematic spectacle.

Client: Directorate General of Sports KP
Project Title: Derajat Off-Road Challenge 2025
Category: Extreme Sports / Action Cinematography
Location: Dera Ismail Khan (DI Khan), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
Delivery Format: High-Energy 4K Highlight Reels, Action Photography, Social Media Promos

Our Role in the Scene

High-Octane Videography
Action Photography
Aggressive FPV & Drone Aerials
Adrenaline-Pumping Video Editing
Immersive Sound Design

Behind the Scenes: Surviving the Dust

Shooting an off-road rally is a completely different beast. You aren't just battling the sun; you are battling the environment. Our crew had to protect the full-frame lenses and cinema rigs from constant clouds of thick dust, flying rocks, and mud splatter, all while constantly moving to stay ahead of the race. It was pure run-and-gun survival mode. We communicated via radios, positioning our ground shooters and drone pilots across the harshest sections of the track to ensure we never missed a crash, a jump, or a victory roar.

Creative Direction

We aimed for a visual style we call "High-Octane Grit." We completely avoided clean, corporate aesthetics. Instead, the color grade was heavily stylized—pushing the warm, dusty tones of the DI Khan desert, crushing the shadows for high contrast, and making the vibrant colors of the rally trucks pop against the barren landscape. The goal was to make the audience feel the grit in their teeth just by watching it.

Results & Reach

Gave a regional off-road challenge the premium, cinematic look of a globally broadcasted motorsport event.

The fast-paced, aggressively edited highlight videos and reels went viral across the province.

Set an entirely new standard for how extreme sports and government-sponsored athletic events are filmed.

Engaged thousands of viewers who couldn't be there in person through a flawless, multi-angle live stream.

Bano Qabil KP Aptitude Test

Capturing Scale & Ambition: The Alkhidmat Bano Qabil KP Mega Tests

About the Project

When you are tasked with documenting one of the largest youth tech initiatives ever held in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, a standard camera on a tripod just won't cut it. The Bano Qabil initiative, powered by the Alkhidmat Foundation, is a massive movement aimed at youth skill-development and IT empowerment.

This wasn't just a single event; it was a province-wide takeover. From the historic backdrop of the Islamia College Ground in Peshawar to the sprawling arenas of the Thana Sports Complex Malakand and Swat Sports Complex, the energy was absolutely electric. Thousands of students, massive crowds, and high stakes across multiple cities. Our mission was to capture the sheer magnitude of this province-wide initiative while keeping the visual narrative personal, inspiring, and cinematic.

Client: Alkhidmat Bano Qabil (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa)
Project Title: The Bano Qabil KP Mega Aptitude Tests
Category: Multi-City Event Production / Live Broadcasting / Brand Identity
Locations: Islamia College Ground (Peshawar), Thana Sports Complex Malakand & Swat Sports Complex
Delivery Format: Multi-Cam Live Streams, 4K Cinematic Highlights

Our Role in the Scene

Multi-City Cinematic Coverage
Epic Aerial Cinematography
Seamless Live Streaming
High-Energy Post-Production
Unified Brand Identity & Design

Behind the Scenes: Managing the Chaos

Covering a multi-city event of this magnitude is a logistical beast. You are dealing with thousands of moving parts, changing natural light across different regions of KP, and the pressure of live broadcasts. Our team operated with strict synchronization. While the live-stream crews were locked into the control stations, our ground operators were constantly hunting for those perfect, candid moments of focus from the youth. Managing gear, syncing footage across cities, and keeping the energy high—it was high-pressure, fast-paced, and exactly the kind of environment we thrive in.

Creative Direction

We wanted the visual identity to feel inspiring and monumental. For the graphic design, we utilized bold typography and high-contrast elements that screamed "opportunity." For the video edit and color grade, we leaned into a bright, dynamic, and high-energy look. We wanted every viewer watching the recaps to feel the adrenaline of the crowd and the ambition of the students, connecting the historic legacy of Peshawar with the rising potential of the Swat region.

Results & Reach

Province-Wide Scale Captured: Successfully documented a massive, multi-city educational movement across KP without missing a beat.

Real-Time Digital Impact: Engaged thousands of viewers through flawless, multi-angle live streams from the different complexes.

Unified Visual Identity: Elevated the Alkhidmat Bano Qabil brand with cohesive, premium graphics.

Viral Highlight Reels: Delivered fast-paced, cinematic recap edits that generated massive organic reach.

Genre-Driven Visuals for a New Age Heritage Movement

About the Project

"The Walled City Chronicles" isn't just another standard travel vlog; it's a cinematic deep-dive into the historical soul of Peshawar. We teamed up with the 2025 Asia Food Battle Champion, Shahzad Khalil, and took him out of the high-energy culinary arenas and into the heart of South Asia's oldest living city.

The goal was simple: pivot his content into a soulful, narrative-driven visual style. We paired his charismatic screen presence with sweeping, movie-like visuals to capture the grandeur of ancient gates, the intricate carved facades, and the echoes of history hidden within the narrowest alleys. We transformed a traditional historical tour into a modern digital odyssey that truly honors the heritage of the Frontier.

Client: Shahzad Khalil
Project Title: Peshawar: The Living History Series
Category: Cultural Documentary / Cinematic Heritage Vlog
Location: The Walled City, Peshawar (Sethi House, Gor Khatri, & Heritage Trail)
Delivery Format: 4K UHD, Cinematic 24fps

Our Role in the Scene

The Cinematic Edit
Pacing & Visual Storytelling
Atmospheric Soundscapes
Neo-Vintage Color Grading
Seamless Drone Integration

Behind the Scenes: Matching the City's Heartbeat

Filming in the dense, historic core of Peshawar requires a run-and-gun mindset and a highly tactical approach to post-production. We were juggling footage from wildly different environments—from the harsh, sun-drenched rooftops of Sethi House to the moody, low-light interiors of ancient caravanserais. Our post-production crew focused on matching the exact heartbeat of the city, using dynamic transitions, natural light leaks, and meticulous color matching to simulate the passage of time across centuries-old structures.

Creative Direction

We called the visual identity for this series "Neo-Noir Heritage." We aggressively avoided the flat, over-exposed look of traditional travelogues. Instead, we utilized deep, moody shadows and rich golden-hour highlights to emphasize the texture and depth of the city. The result is a visual tribute that treats Peshawar's architecture not just as a beautiful backdrop, but as a living, breathing character in the story.

Results & Reach

40K+ views in the first week of release and massive cross-platform engagement.

Reached a massive, diverse international audience, sparking renewed interest in Peshawar's historic district.

Shared widely on TikTok & Instagram with organic viral reach and engagement.

Delivered significant increase in cross-platform followers hungry for cinematic storytelling.
